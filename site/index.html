First time opening it on a Mac

We're a small team and the app isn't registered with Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Megan Study into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

Says the app is "damaged", or there's no Open Anyway button? Open Terminal, paste this, and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Megan Study.app"

On an Apple silicon Mac you can skip all of that and install from Terminal instead:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/neftyhot/study-app/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
